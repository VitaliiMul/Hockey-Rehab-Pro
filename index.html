The Process

Your Road Back to the Ice

Three phases. One proven system. Built for hockey.

01

Protect & Restore

Phase 1 — Weeks 0–12

Reduce swelling, restore range of motion, rebuild quad activation, and establish your strength foundation.

02

Rebuild Strength & Power

Phase 2 — Months 3–6

Progressive loading, single-leg benchmarks, lateral stability, and explosive power — all cleared with objective testing.

03

Return to Ice

Phase 3 — Months 6–12+

Hockey-specific skating mechanics, edge work, battle drills, and sport-specific benchmarks that tell you when you're truly ready.
